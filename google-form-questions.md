# Free One-Hour Personalised Demo - Google Form Questions

Use these fields for the booking/intake form.

## Contact Details

1. Business name
2. Business address
3. Business website
4. Contact person
5. Contact person's role/job title
6. Contact email address
7. Contact phone number

## Business Profile

8. What type of business are you?
9. How many people are in your team?
10. What services/products do you provide?
11. Who are your main customers?

## Current Communication

12. How does your business use email day to day?
13. Which email platform do you use? Example: Gmail, Microsoft 365, Outlook, other.
14. Do you use WhatsApp for business communication?
15. Do you receive customer enquiries by phone?
16. Which social media platforms do you use?
17. How often do you post or respond on social media?

## Automation Opportunities

18. Which daily admin tasks repeat the most?
19. Which workflows slow your team down?
20. What would you most like a personal assistant to handle?
21. Do you need help with customer follow-ups?
22. Do you need help with inbox triage?
23. Do you need help with appointments, scheduling, or reminders?
24. Do you need help creating apps, dashboards, or internal tools?

## Demo Preparation

25. What is one workflow you would like us to look at during the free demo?
26. What tools does your team already use?
27. What would make the demo successful for you?
28. Preferred date/time for the one-hour demo
